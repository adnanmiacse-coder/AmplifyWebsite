Platform Features

Built to replace passive learning

Every tool here exists to make a student genuinely understand — not just pass.

Adaptive AI

Personalised AI Tutor

The tutor learns your pace, weak areas, and learning style. It generates live animations from lesson topics, runs contextual quizzes, and delivers improvement suggestions based on your actual performance — not a generic syllabus.

Second Brain

Full Knowledge System

Topic breakdowns, spaced repetition scheduling, and an attention tracker that surfaces what you're drifting away from before exams.

Offline

NCTB Offline Mode

Ask any question and receive answers sourced directly from NCTB textbooks — no internet, no image uploads, no hallucination risk. Just accurate curriculum answers, always available.

Einstein Mode

Advanced Research Assistance

A multimodal research layer for students ready to go beyond the textbook — from foundational concepts to advanced academic material, with visual context at every step.

3D Lab

3D Practical Animations

Science practicals, diagrams, and spatial concepts rendered in 3D with personalised tutoring. Navigate with voice or gesture — no lab equipment required.

Classroom AI

AI-Powered Classroom System

Three AI student models — Curious, Skeptic, and Achiever — participate in real-time classroom sessions alongside real students, raising the quality of discussion in classrooms across rural and urban regions alike. Teachers get a full ecosystem: attendance, announcements, assignments, and live engagement data.

Focus

Focus Monitoring

Tracks attention levels using facial feature analysis during study sessions. Identifies drift patterns and nudges the student back — passively, without interrupting flow.

Wellbeing

Daily Mind Check-in

Track mood, stress, and study energy daily. Smart scheduling adjusts around your mental state so you're never pushed to grind through the wrong sessions.
